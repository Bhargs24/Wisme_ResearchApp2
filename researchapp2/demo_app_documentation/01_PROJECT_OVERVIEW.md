# 📱 **WISME RESEARCH DEMO APP - PROJECT OVERVIEW**

## 🎯 **Purpose**
Dual-purpose research platform to validate Wisme's conversational learning method while collecting investor-grade data for ₹60 crore pre-deployment valuation.

## 🔬 **Research Objectives**
1. **Learning Method Validation**: Prove conversational learning superiority
2. **Academic Research**: Generate IEEE conference-level papers
3. **Investor Data**: Collect metrics supporting high valuation
4. **Market Validation**: Demonstrate user engagement and retention

## 📊 **Success Metrics**
- **Engagement**: 3x higher than traditional methods
- **Retention**: 85%+ knowledge retention after 1 week
- **User Preference**: 80%+ prefer conversational over traditional
- **Willingness to Pay**: ₹500-1000/month subscription intent

## 🎯 **Target Audience**
- **Primary**: Engineering college students (DSA, Psychology)
- **Secondary**: General audience (Personal Finance, Science Mysteries)
- **Research Participants**: 500+ users across 4 learning journeys

## ⏱️ **Timeline**
- **Development**: 4 weeks
- **Content Production**: 8 weeks (21 episodes)
- **Research Phase**: 12 weeks
- **Data Analysis**: 4 weeks
- **Paper Publication**: 8 weeks

## 🎧 **Content Strategy**
**Conversational-Only Approach**: No traditional method comparison during learning experience. Users reflect on their existing learning methods through surveys before and after experiencing pure Wisme conversational episodes.

## 💰 **Investment Validation**
Research data will support ₹60 crore pre-deployment valuation through:
- User engagement metrics
- Learning effectiveness data  
- Market demand validation
- Competitive advantage proof

## 🛠️ Update: Journey Scope Change
- The demo app now focuses on 4 core journeys: DSA, Psychology, Science Mysteries, and Personal Finance.
- OS, DBMS, Computer Networks, Marketing, and Productivity journeys are removed for this phase.
- Total journeys: 4 (21 episodes total)
- Focus is on method validation, not comparing different subjects against each other.
