# Conversational-Only Strategy for the Demo App

## Why Conversational-Only?
- The research demo focuses exclusively on conversational learning to isolate and scientifically validate its effectiveness compared to traditional methods.
- This approach ensures that all user feedback and learning data are attributable to the conversational method, supporting clear, publishable research outcomes.

## Research Purity
- By avoiding mixed-method content in the demo, we eliminate confounding variables and maximize the credibility of our findings for academic and investor audiences.

## Full App Vision
- The full Wisme app will expand to include additional learning modes, adaptive content, and more advanced AI-driven personalization.
- The demo is a focused research tool; the full product will be a comprehensive, multi-modal learning platform.
