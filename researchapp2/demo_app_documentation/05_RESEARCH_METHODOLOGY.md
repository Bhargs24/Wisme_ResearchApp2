## 🔍 **Research Design & Controls**

### **Randomization Strategy**
- **Journey Assignment**: Random distribution across all 7 journeys
- **Episode Sequence**: Some users get randomized episode order
- **Survey Timing**: Varied delay between episode and feedback
- **Control Groups**: 10% get traditional content for comparison

### **Bias Mitigation**
- **Leading Questions**: Carefully worded neutral survey questions
- **Social Desirability**: Anonymous data collection option
- **Selection Bias**: Diverse recruitment across multiple channels
- **Confirmation Bias**: Pre-registered hypothesis and analysis plan

### **Data Quality Assurance**
- **Attention Checks**: Hidden validation questions in surveys
- **Duplicate Prevention**: Device fingerprinting and email verification
- **Response Time Monitoring**: Flag extremely fast survey completions
- **Outlier Detection**: Statistical analysis for unusual response patterns
